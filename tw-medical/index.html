Help Center

Answers to common questions

Ask on WhatsApp
How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 2–5 business days depending on your pincode. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available in major cities. Orders above ₹499 ship free.

What is your return policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Medicines and opened hygiene products are non-returnable unless damaged or incorrect.

Are all products genuine?

Yes. We source directly from manufacturers and licensed distributors, and every batch is checked for expiry and authenticity.

Do I need a prescription?

Prescription (Rx) medicines require a valid prescription, which you can share at checkout or on WhatsApp. Devices and supplies do not need one.
